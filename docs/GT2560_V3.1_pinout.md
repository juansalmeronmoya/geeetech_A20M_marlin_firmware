# GT2560 V3.1 pinout

Board: Geeetech GT2560 V3.1 (ATmega2560). The same PCB is used on the A10M, A20M and A30M
(1, 2 and 3 extruders); on the A20M the `EXT2` footprint is fully populated except for the
connector itself.

Official schematic (V3.0, same layout as the V3.1):
[GT2560_V3.0_SCH.pdf](https://github.com/Geeetech3D/Diagram/blob/master/GT2560_V3.0_SCH.pdf)

## Extruder connectors `EXT0` / `EXT1` / `EXT2` (2x6)

Same pinout on the three connectors, `n` being the extruder number.

| Pin | Signal         | Pin | Signal              |
|-----|----------------|-----|---------------------|
| 1   | Motor `1A`     | 2   | Motor `1B`          |
| 3   | Motor `2B`     | 4   | Motor `2A`          |
| 5   | VDC            | 6   | Fan `FAN_En-`       |
| 7   | VDC            | 8   | GND                 |
| 9   | VDC            | 10  | Heater `HEn-`       |
| 11  | GND thermistor | 12  | Thermistor `Tn`     |

On the original harness the hotend thermistor of `EXT0` uses the orange and white wires
(~100 kΩ between them at room temperature).

## Heated bed connector `HB` (2x3)

| Pin | Signal          | Pin | Signal |
|-----|-----------------|-----|--------|
| 1   | `12V_BED`       | 2   | `12V_BED` |
| 3   | Bed thermistor `T_BED` | 4 | GND |
| 5   | `BED-`          | 6   | `BED-` |

Note that the bed thermistor sits right next to the bed power pins.

## Thermistor inputs

Every thermistor input has a 4.7 kΩ pull-up to 5 V and a 10 µF filter capacitor to GND.

| Signal  | Connector       | Schematic (MCU pin) | Marlin `pins_GT2560_V3.h` |
|---------|-----------------|---------------------|---------------------------|
| `T0`    | `EXT0` pin 12   | PK0 / A8 (89)       | `TEMP_0_PIN` = A11        |
| `T1`    | `EXT1` pin 12   | PK1 / A9 (88)       | `TEMP_1_PIN` = A9         |
| `T2`    | `EXT2` pin 12   | PK3 / A11 (86)      | `TEMP_2_PIN` = A8         |
| `T_BED` | `HB` pin 3      | PK2 / A10 (87)      | `TEMP_BED_PIN` = A10      |

The schematic and Marlin disagree on `T0` / `T2` (A8 vs A11). The hotend on `EXT0` has always
worked with Marlin's `TEMP_0_PIN` = A11, so Marlin's mapping is the one that matches the real
board, and `EXT2` is A8.

## Bed thermistor rewiring (October 2026)

While drying a filament spool on the heated bed, the aluminium foil around it shorted the
uninsulated connectors under the bed. The short pushed bed power into the bed thermistor input:

- The thermistor itself is fine (104 kΩ disconnected).
- The `HB` thermistor input now shows a permanent ~0.56 kΩ leak to GND, so Marlin reads a
  fixed ~108-110 °C even with nothing connected. Replacing the filter capacitor did not fix it.

Workaround: the bed thermistor is moved to the thermistor pins of `EXT2` (pins 11-12), which
keeps `EXT1` free for a future second hotend.

- `EXT2` has no connector soldered: solder a connector or the two wires to pads 11 and 12.
- The thermistor has no polarity.
- Before connecting it, with the board unpowered: pad 11 must have continuity to GND and pad 12
  must read ~4.7 kΩ to 5 V (wait a few seconds, the 10 µF capacitor has to charge).
- After flashing, the bed must read room temperature (~20-25 °C).

Firmware changes, applied to both `A20M_Marlin_2.0.9.5` and `A20M_Marlin_2.1.2.8`:

- `Marlin/src/pins/mega/pins_GT2560_V3.h`: `TEMP_BED_PIN` set to `TEMP_2_PIN` (original `10`
  kept as a comment).
- `Marlin/Configuration.h`: single extruder for now (`EXTRUDERS 1`, `TEMP_SENSOR_1 0`,
  `NUM_RUNOUT_SENSORS 1`). To add the second hotend on `EXT1` later, revert these three values;
  `TEMP_1_PIN` does not clash with the bed.
